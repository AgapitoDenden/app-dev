# My favorite movie 

## John Wick

**John Wick** is a stylish neo-noir action film about a retired hitman forced back into the criminal underworld after a personal loss. Known for its tight storytelling, “gun-fu” choreography, and immersive world-building, it redefined modern action cinema.

### Highlights
- **Action style:** Precise “gun-fu,” close-quarters combat, and long, fluid takes  
- **World-building:** The Continental, gold coins, codes of conduct, and assassin hierarchy  
- **Tone:** Cool, minimalist dialogue with relentless momentum and emotional stakes  

### Main characters
- **John Wick:** Legendary hitman seeking vengeance  
- **Winston:** Continental’s enigmatic manager  
- **Charon:** Professional and composed concierge  
- **Viggo Tarasov:** Crime boss tied to John’s past

### Why I like it
1. **Choreography:** Clean, readable fights with practical stunts  
2. **Aesthetic:** Sleek lighting, sharp suits, and a pulsing score  
3. **Mythology:** A fully realized underworld with unique rules and symbols

### Favorite quote
> "People keep asking if I'm back. Yeah, I'm thinkin' I'm back."
